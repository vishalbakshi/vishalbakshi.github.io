What I Do

Problem formulation

I turn business knowledge into a technical approach your SMEs can validate.

I focus on three questions using production data:

  1. What needs to happen and why?
  2. How do we implement it?
  3. Does the implementation do what the business needs?

Cross-functional alignment

I make our reasoning visible so we can question and correct it together.

  • I write down what I understand so you can confirm or correct it.
  • I ask questions with the relevant teammates present so we can learn from each other’s explanations.
  • When explanations differ, I escalate them so we can understand the nuances of your business operations.

Scaling the team’s capacity

My delivery focuses on implementing systems of people and technology that help your team continuously improve how they formulate problems and implement solutions together. These systems usually involve human-written documentation, individual and group training sessions, and human-written and tested agent skills.

Case Study

Modernizing a national shipment rescheduling system.

Your shipment rescheduling system is based on an Excel workbook with a dozen macros with no documentation. You want to modernize it as a FastAPI backend with a business rule decision engine. The lead engineer left and the project is blocked. You come across my profile, schedule an intro call, and deem us a fit. You parachute me in.

Each day I collaborate with logistics experts to understand what happens on the warehouse floor, across databases, and in legacy software from 1993. I then implement that into code we verify the next day.

Three weeks in, I begin onboarding your new engineer. Together, we build the MVP that uses real-time data to make real decisions on real shipments, with an inspectable trace your experts can review. As they take ownership, I shift into an advisory role. They bring the system into production, serving dozens of carriers and thousands of shipments every day.
